## Selected Projects

### [Evolutionary Hyperparameter Optimization for Multi-Layer Perceptron](https://github.com/LeiPan-JLU/evolutionary-hpo-mlp)

A reproducible comparison of Random Search, real-coded GA, LDIW-PSO, DE/rand/1/bin,
and jDE for tuning a two-layer MLP on the `digits` dataset. The search space has four
mixed continuous/integer hyperparameters and is evaluated by 5-fold cross-validation.
Across 20 independent runs, real-coded GA achieved the lowest mean error (0.0213),
while jDE's individual parameter adaptation did not outperform fixed-parameter DE at
the 400-evaluation budget.

`Python` · `NumPy` · `SciPy` · `scikit-learn` · `Matplotlib`
